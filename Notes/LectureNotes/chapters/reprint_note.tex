\markboth{Note on This Study Reprint}{}
This document is a locally prepared study reprint of Frank W. Warner's
\emph{Foundations of Differentiable Manifolds and Lie Groups}, Graduate Texts
in Mathematics 94. The source supplied with this repository is a scanned
copy catalogued as a 2000 printing. Its OCR transcription was the starting
point for this typesetting; the original author's and editor's prefaces
are reproduced separately below.

Chapters 1--6 retain Warner's numbered items (for example, 1.2 and 5.17).
Numbered discussions are part of that same sequence, not additional
subsections. Equation numbers in these chapters retain the original local
numbering within an item. Thus ``(4)'' means the fourth numbered equation
in the current item unless another item is specified. Page numbers refer
to this reprint and differ from those of the scanned book.

The revision dated 16 September 2026 restores these numbering conventions,
replaces the embedded scan figures with editable vector drawings, revises
page layout, corrects identified transcription errors, and rebuilds the
notation index with links to the corresponding numbered discussions.
Drawings are schematic and need not reproduce the original geometry to scale.

Appendices A and B are editorial teaching supplements, not chapters from
Warner's book. Appendix A was already present in the supplied project; its
figure captions identify the reference cited there. Appendix B has been
completed with statements and proofs of the implicit and constant-rank
theorems and supporting linear algebra. These additions are distinguished
from the author's text by their appendix numbering.

The source, figure inventory, and verification instructions are included
in the repository so that further corrections can be traced and rebuilt.
This revision does not claim an independent proof audit of every result
or a word-for-word critical collation of the entire book. When reporting a
suspected error, give the chapter, original item number, and formula or
figure concerned; these identifiers remain stable as pagination changes.
